About Midwest Diesel Core

Midwest Diesel Core is a family-owned business based in the ChicagoLand area, specializing in buying and selling light- and heavy-duty core parts. We offer competitive core pricing, helping customers find the components they need and get value from the parts they sell.

The Core Parts You Need

We buy and sell a wide range of light- and heavy-duty components including engine parts, powersteering pumps, medium- and heavy-duty brake calipers, diesel injectors, and diesel particulated filters (DPFs). We also handle complete engines and transmissions, engine control modules (ECMs), and light- and heavy-duty starts and alternators.
