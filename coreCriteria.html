Before You Sell

A clear description starts the conversation.

Core value depends on the specific part and its condition. Before arranging delivery, provide available part numbers, quantities, photographs, and a description of any damage or missing components.

  • Photograph identification labels, casting numbers, and the overall condition.
  • Tell us whether the unit is assembled, partially dismantled, or missing pieces.
  • Disclose cracks, broken mounts, corrosion, fire exposure, water intrusion, or prior repairs.
  • Keep associated components together and avoid additional disassembly before review.
Unsure whether a part qualifies?

Contact us before shipping. A description or photograph can help us discuss the next step without you sending an uncertain item.

Diesel injectors and pump assemblies

Include the manufacturer and part number when available. For injector assemblies, identify whether you have individual injectors or a complete assembly with lines and connectors.

  • Describe damage to bodies, tips, fittings, electrical terminals, or mounting points.
  • Identify missing solenoids, valves, plugs, fasteners, hoses, or related pieces.
  • Mention seized components, contamination, or known internal damage.
  • For power steering pumps, include information about the shaft, housing, ports, and any attached reservoir.

Medium- and heavy-duty brake calipers

Provide casting numbers or application details and clear photographs of the caliper assembly. Note whether associated brackets and hardware are included.

  • Point out cracked castings, broken mounting ears, or damaged threaded openings.
  • Disclose missing internal or external components.
  • Describe heavy corrosion, seized mechanisms, or visible heat damage.

ECMs, starters, and alternators

Identification is especially helpful for electrical cores. Send readable photographs of labels and connectors, along with any known operating issues.

  • Note broken terminals, cut wiring, damaged connector pins, or cracked housings.
  • Disclose water intrusion, burn marks, substantial corrosion, or opened cases.
  • For starters and alternators, describe missing pulleys, gears, covers, or other pieces.
  • Include the voltage rating or vehicle application if known.

DPFs and catalytic converter combs

Identify whether you have a complete assembly or loose ceramic material. Include available identification numbers and photographs of the housing and visible substrate.

  • Describe cracked, crumbling, melted, or missing ceramic material.
  • Disclose cut housings, previous modifications, and known contamination.
  • For loose combs, provide approximate dimensions and the number of pieces.
  • Avoid breaking or altering material to prepare it for a quote.

Discuss the material with our team before packing so its condition and shipping needs can be reviewed.

Engines, transmissions, and related components

Include the make, model, application, and identification numbers where possible. Explain whether the unit is complete and describe its known failure or removal reason.

  • Identify holes, cracks, broken mounts, or damage to major castings.
  • List missing accessories, covers, internal parts, or attached components.
  • Disclose seizure, overheating, fire exposure, or water intrusion.
  • Coordinate handling and freight details before moving large assemblies.

Have damaged parts or something not listed?

Send us the details rather than assuming a part has no value. Explain the damage and include photographs so our team can discuss whether it is a purchasing fit.

These details help with an initial review; they do not guarantee acceptance or a particular price. Confirm the applicable condition requirements and transaction terms with our team before shipping.

Once the details are confirmed, review our Shipping Guidelines for preparation information.
