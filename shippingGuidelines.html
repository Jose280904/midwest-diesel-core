Freight packaging requirement

Freight shipments must arrive in a Gaylord box on a red or blue pallet. If you cannot meet this requirement, contact our team before booking your shipment.

Use a red or blue pallet with a Gaylord box.

For freight shipments, place your cores inside a sturdy Gaylord box supported by a red or blue pallet. The pallet and box must be suitable for the combined weight of the parts.

Prepare the Base

  • Use a red or blue pallet that is free of broken boards or other structural damage.
  • Position the Gaylord box within the pallet’s edges without overhang.
  • Line the bottom as needed to keep small parts from slipping through openings.
  • Keep forklift entry points clear and accessible.

Arrange and Protect the Parts

  • Place heavier components at the bottom and spread the weight evenly across the pallet.
  • Cushion or separate fragile components, especially ceramic combs and exposed connectors.
  • Prevent parts from moving, rolling, or striking one another during transport.
  • Keep the shipment within the rated capacity of the pallet and Gaylord box.

Secure the Completed Load

  • Use appropriate straps or banding to secure the box and its contents to the pallet.
  • Cover the top to help protect the contents during handling and transportation.
  • Apply stretch wrap as appropriate, keeping labels and forklift access clear.
  • Do not rely on stretch wrap alone to hold heavy components in place.

Confirm carrier arrangements, shipment dimensions, total weight, and any loading requirements with our team before scheduling pickup.

Discuss small parcel shipments with our team.

For smaller quantities, contact us to confirm whether boxed parcel shipping is appropriate. If approved, use packaging suited to the parts and the carrier’s weight limits.

  • Choose a strong shipping box rated for the full weight of its contents.
  • Wrap components individually and fill empty space to prevent movement.
  • Protect fittings, electrical connections, mounting points, and other vulnerable areas.
  • Keep brittle ceramic material separate from heavy metal components.
  • Seal and reinforce box seams with suitable packing tape.
  • Use additional boxes or discuss freight shipping if the load exceeds packaging or carrier limits.

Check with your carrier about preparation requirements for parts containing residual fluids or other materials that could affect shipment acceptance.

Include the information needed to identify your shipment.

Clear labels and an accurate parts list help connect your shipment to the correct inquiry or transaction.

  • Include your full name, company name if applicable, phone number, and return address.
  • Add an inventory of the parts, including quantities and available part numbers.
  • Include any quote, purchase order, or reference number supplied by our team.
  • Number multiple packages or pallets so the complete shipment can be identified.
  • Attach required freight documents in a visible, protected location.
  • Give these details to anyone preparing or shipping parts on your behalf.

Keep a Record Before Pickup

Photograph the parts, the packing arrangement, and the secured shipment. Retain copies of your inventory, tracking information, and freight paperwork for reference.

Coordinate delivery and confirm the next steps.

Share tracking or freight information with Midwest Diesel Core so delivery can be coordinated. Confirm receiving arrangements before sending a carrier to our location.

If a shipment is delayed, damaged, or missing items, contact our team and retain the carrier documents and packaging photographs.

Inspection, payment, and any damage-related arrangements should be confirmed before shipping. Carrier claim procedures depend on the shipping service and agreement used.
